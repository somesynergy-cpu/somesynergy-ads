SoMe Synergy Ads Manager

An internal tool used by the SoMe Synergy marketing agency to manage and report on the Google Ads accounts of its clients.

What the tool does

  • Reads campaign, ad group, product and conversion performance data from Google Ads accounts linked to our manager account (MCC).
  • Creates and updates campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, negative keywords, budgets and audiences on behalf of our clients, after the change is reviewed and approved by an agency specialist.
  • Prepares monthly performance reports for clients (spend, conversions, conversion value, return on ad spend).

Who uses it

The tool is used only by employees of SoMe Synergy. It is not offered to the public and has no self-service sign-up. It accesses only Google Ads accounts that our clients have linked to the SoMe Synergy manager account.

How it connects to Google

The tool uses the Google Ads API with OAuth 2.0 (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). Authorization is granted by an agency employee signed in with the agency's Google account. Details on what data we access and how we protect it are in our Privacy Policy.
